# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background, identity or country. We pledge to keep this project open, friendly and harassment-free.

## Our standards

Good behavior:
- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent and ask when unsure.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Trolling, spam or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may remove people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the repository's issues or the email on their GitHub profile.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
